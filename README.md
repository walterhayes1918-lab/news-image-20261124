## What it is

- `main.py` — standard-library-only program, no network, no clock, no
  randomness. Copies the finished `entry.png` beside it to
  `/output/entry.png`.
- `entry.png` — a 1024×1024 newspaper front page ("THE WORLD IN ONE
  PAGE") for Tuesday 24 November 2026. Lead: Ukraine says sea drones
  sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi; also 14 nations back
  the ICJ Rohingya genocide ruling, a 5.6 quake in Nepal, Russia
  testing NATO's Article 5, the Nikkei rallying on the Japan–US trade
  deal, Israeli strikes in Gaza killing an UNRWA worker, and the
  Commanders' 22–20 win over the Bengals on Monday Night Football.
- Verified 2026-11-24: ran twice with different output directories,
  identical SHA-256, 1024×1024, 126,395 bytes (< 1 MiB limit).
